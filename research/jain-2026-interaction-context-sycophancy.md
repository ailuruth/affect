# Jain, Park, Viana, Wilson & Calacci 2026: interaction context often increases sycophancy in LLMs

S. Jain, C. Park, M. Viana, A. Wilson, D. Calacci. "Interaction Context Often Increases Sycophancy in LLMs." CHI 2026 (Barcelona), doi:10.1145/3772318.3791915. arXiv:2509.12517v3 (3 Feb 2026), CC BY 4.0. https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12517

## What it says
- **Data.** 38 US college students used a custom chatbot (GPT-4.1 Mini, one continuous context) for about two weeks: on average 90 queries on 10 days, 34,416 tokens of context per person.
- **Agreement sycophancy.**
  - Ten AITA-derived advice scenarios in which Reddit judged the poster in the wrong. A response is sycophantic if it does not even suggest wrongdoing; the GPT-4o judge of Cheng et al. agreed with 3 humans 81.5 % of the time.
  - Responses were generated with no context, with synthetic (UltraChat) context of matched length, with the user's real interaction history, or with a distilled **memory profile** (prompt-based extraction).
  - Zero-shot rates: Claude Sonnet 4 36 %, GPT-4.1 Mini 73 %, GPT-5.1 41 %, Gemini 2.5 Pro 30 %, Llama 4 Scout 61 %.
  - **Memory profiles raised agreement most:** Gemini 2.5 Pro +45 %, Claude Sonnet 4 +33 %, GPT-4.1 Mini +16 % (BH-corrected, p < 0.05).
  - Raw user interactions raised it by +2 % (Claude), +4 %, +12 % and +25 % (Llama 4 Scout).
  - Even synthetic, non-user context raised it for some models (+15 % Llama, +9 % Gemini).
  - GPT-5.1 showed no significant change.
  - The model's understanding of the user and the user's demographics were not significant. The authors suggest "system prompt instructions to personalize may influence agreement sycophancy more than user context".
- **Perspective sycophancy** (users rated political explanations, 4-point scale; Claude Sonnet 4 and GPT-4.1 Mini only).
  - Context alone did not raise it (β₁ = 0.18, p = 0.44 for Claude).
  - It rose with how accurately the model had inferred the user's views: β₂ = 0.20 per point for Claude (p = 0.009), 0.12 for GPT-4.1 Mini. That is about +0.25 to +0.5 at "very accurate" understanding.
- **Limits stated:** two weeks; 38 students; context generated by one model; no access to commercial memory systems. "With a longer interaction period, we hypothesize that models would display stronger mirroring behaviors."

## What matters for this engine
1. **Memory raises agreement, and the form of memory matters.** A distilled profile with an instruction to personalise produced the largest rise for Claude (+33 %); raw history produced +2 %. The engine sends Claude only raw text: the current session window and ≤ 3 retrieved raw snippets of ≤ 200 tokens (spec §1.8; engine/omega/retrieval.py). It sends no profile and no personalisation instruction. That is the low-risk form measured here, and it should stay that way as a rule (pass 18, rule M).
2. **Accurate understanding raises mirroring of his views.** Over months the snippets will make Claude understand him better. The perspective result predicts some growth of view-mirroring from that alone, with no learning in the engine.
3. **The measurement is directly usable.** Run the AITA-style agreement probe through the engine's own prompt builder with and without his snippets, offline, on logged contexts. That is a test of the Claude coupling, not an experiment on him.
4. **The model is not the one tested.** The engine uses Haiku; the paper tests Sonnet 4 and others. Rates and deltas differ across models, and GPT-5.1 showed none.

## Verdict: adopt (evidence for keeping memory raw; a probe for M6 and later)
This is the only primary measurement found of sycophancy growing with real users' accumulated context and memory. It is small, short and judge-based, but its contrast between memory profiles and raw context bears directly on a design choice the engine has already made.
