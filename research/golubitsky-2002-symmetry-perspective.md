The twisted-intertwiner bound is proved directly in `symmetry-no-copy-synthesis.md` and checked numerically (C6).

The H/K theorem on spatio-temporal symmetry (anti-phase solutions read as "copy at a half-period lag") was mentioned in the earlier draft. **It is not verified here**, and the synthesis relies instead on the numeric rotation-as-delay check (C2).
