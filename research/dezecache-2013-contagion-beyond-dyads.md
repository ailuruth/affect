# Dezecache, Conty, Chadwick, Philip, Soussignan, Sperber & Grèzes 2013, "Evidence for Unintentional Emotional Contagion Beyond Dyads" (PLoS ONE 8(6):e67371, doi 10.1371/journal.pone.0067371)

Link: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3696100/ (open access).

## What was read
The full PMC text: introduction, methods of both experiments, results, discussion, figure captions.

## What it says
- **Design.** A chain A → B → C. A: 45 videos (6 s) of actors showing joy or fear with face, body and voice, or landscapes (15 each). B: 60 women watched them with headphones and did not know they were observed; a webcam relayed B's face to C. C: 30 men watched B's face only (no sound of A), with the task of guessing what B had seen. EMG of C's zygomaticus and corrugator (maximum change 0.5–6 s after onset) and skin conductance. Usable: zygomaticus n = 23, corrugator n = 17, SCR n = 19.
- **Same-sign transfer two steps away.** C's zygomaticus was higher when B watched joy than landscapes (d = 0.45) or fear (d = 0.62); C's corrugator was higher when B watched fear than landscapes (d = 0.68) or joy (d = 0.53). C's skin conductance rose for both joy and fear compared with landscapes (d ≈ 0.25), not differing between them.
- **Without explicit recognition (for joy).** Three naive judges labelling B's recorded faces (609 clips) were at chance for joy and above chance for fear and landscapes (κ = 0.78). The authors note that C saw only two B's while the judges saw many, so C's sensitivity may differ.
- The protocol was "not designed" to tell whether C's reactions are motor mimicry or appraisal; B's own physiology was not recorded.

## What matters for this engine
- **Gap 8 (the leftover same-sign transfer).** This is same-sign facial and autonomic transfer through faces whose content observers could not name. It does not separate a direct transfer path from social appraisal: C's only evidence about the event was B's face, and C's task was to infer that event. That is the social-referencing case of social-appraisal-synthesis.md §4, point 3 (the face is the only evidence about the shared event), where appraisal and contagion predict the same matching. It shows that very weak cues suffice, not which route carries them.
- **Per expression.** The transferred patterns were specific on the face (cheek to joy, brow to fear) and unspecific in arousal, which fits a fast per-channel match plus a general activation.
- **Learning signal:** none; no learning is studied.
- **For the build core.** The fast echo gives per-channel same-sign transfer at 0.45 s, capped at G_k (0.64 c on lip pull, 0.60 c on cheek raise, 0.10 c on inner brow), and a frown gets no frown back (BL zero ceiling). The agent reads his face for knowing (Π̃, H_id), and his read state raises only its care load (C_F), which reaches no display. No source here forces more.

## Limits
Sex confound by design (B women, C men); one B per C session at a time; maximum-change scoring over a 5.5-s window; small usable samples per measure; judges and C differ in exposure.

## Verdict: adopt (as evidence that same-sign facial transfer survives very weak, unnamed cues; it does not decide between transfer and social referencing)
