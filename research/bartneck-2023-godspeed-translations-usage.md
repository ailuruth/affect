# Bartneck 2023, "Godspeed Questionnaire Series: Translations and Usage" (in Krägeloh, Medvedev & Alyami (eds), International Handbook of Behavioral Health Assessment, Springer, pp. 1-35, doi 10.1007/978-3-030-89738-3_24-1)

Source read: accepted-version PDF from bartneck.de (39 pages, metadata dated Feb 2023), sections 1-8 in full (history, development, subsequent psychometrics, the 19 translation sections, cultural considerations, scoring, limitations, conclusion). Did not read the appendix figures that reproduce each translation, or the full reference list.

## Claims
- The GQS is a "series since it consists of five scales that can each be used as a stand-alone questionnaires. The GQS is not intended to be always used with all of its questions, particularly due to the overlap between anthropomorphism and animacy ... researchers should pick the concept most relevant to their own research."
- Scoring (section 6): "Participants are asked to mark their answer on a five point scale that is anchored with opposing terms ... The task is made easier if all the positive terms are located on the right and receive the highest score. Researchers might want to reverse the order of the terms ... but then the researchers also need to be extra careful to reverse the scores in the data analysis ... An average score for each of the concepts can be taken to represent that particular concept."
- Overlap fix: "only one of the concepts at a time should be included in each research study", or show Artificial/Lifelike twice and keep the repeats apart when randomising.
- Reporting: researchers "should report which language version of the GQS was used"; "It is highly advisable to use the forth-and-back method" for translation; users should report "internal consistency, but also reliability and validity".
- A Korean translation exists (Elena Knox / Jenna Lee, 2018): "They did not report on any psychometric properties of the Korean scales. Chung and Shin (2015) reported on Cronbach's alpha values of well above 0.70 for the Korean scales." (We did not check which translation Chung and Shin used.)

## Evidence
- Ho & MacDorman (2010), as summarised here: likeability and anthropomorphism alpha "around 0.92", animacy and perceived intelligence "around 0.88", "Only the Cronbach's alpha for perceived safety was below (0.6)".
- Stroessner (2020) ranges as quoted: anthropomorphism 0.86-0.93, animacy 0.70-0.76, likeability 0.84-0.92, perceived intelligence 0.75-0.92, perceived safety 0.91.
- Weiss & Bartneck (2015) meta-review: of 160 citing papers, "only 69 conducted empirical research with the questionnaire series", and "only 21 of them used the same robotic hardware. Hence it remained difficult to draw conclusions by comparing the results of those 21 studies."
- Conclusion: "the Perceived Safety internal consistency is often the lowest amongst the five concepts."
- Limitations: true reliability needs repeated experiments ("To establish true reliability, the same experiment would need to be repeated several times"). On sensitivity: "Many concepts under investigations in HRI have a medium effect at best. Hence it is not uncommon to observe a floor effect in which the measurement instrument is not sensitive enough to pick up the small differences."
- Successor scales are named (RoSAS, Ho & MacDorman indices, HRIES, PeRDITA, Trust Perception Scale-HRI). Their "uptake is still below that of the GQS".

## Verdict: adopt (as the usage rules for Godspeed)
This chapter settles three points the 2009 paper left open: (1) scoring is the per-scale mean, with any reversed-order items reversed back; (2) use Anthropomorphism or Animacy, not both, or duplicate Artificial/Lifelike; (3) state the language version and how it was translated. If participants are Korean, use the collected Korean translation and report it as such. Its psychometrics are unreported, so compute and report alpha. Perceived Safety's alpha has come in as low as about 0.6, so a null or noisy Safety result is weak evidence either way. Cross-study comparisons are unreliable (only 21 of 69 studies shared hardware), so compare only within our own design and never against published means.
