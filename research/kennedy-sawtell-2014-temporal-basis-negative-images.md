## The computation, precisely
- **Problem.** The fish's own electric discharge (EOD) rings in passive electroreceptors "for as long as the interval between EODs", masking external fields. The brain must subtract the self-caused part and keep the externally caused part.
- **Mechanism, now complete at circuit level.**
  1. A copy of the brief motor command (corollary discharge) reaches granule cells through mossy fibres. Extrinsic fibres fire only at short delays ("early", "medium"); unipolar brush cells (UBCs) generate "late" (> 50 ms, rebound bursts) and "pause" (tonic firing stopped at the command, then time-locked) responses.
  2. Each granule cell sums a *random* mixture of about three such inputs: the fitted mixtures match "a four-parameter random mixing model … in which each input to a granule cell is assigned independently". Fits: average MSE 4.6%, n = 169.
  3. A medium ganglion cell receives ~20,000 granule-cell inputs through plastic synapses with "non-associative potentiation" per presynaptic spike and "associative depression" when a postsynaptic spike follows a presynaptic one (anti-Hebbian). Over "about 1000 EOD commands (approximately 5 minutes)" the synaptic input becomes a **negative image** of the self-caused sensory input and cancels it, with command-to-command s.d. about 1 mV.
- **What remains after cancellation is, by construction, the part of the input not predictable from the animal's own command history.** The cancellation is learned, not wired, and it is specific to what is self-caused.

## Evidence with numbers
- Corollary-discharge responses in 170 of 184 granule cells; ~20% fire, 1.25 spikes/command; distance to threshold 20.2 ± 5.97 mV.
- **The basis is matched to the self-signal, not general.** Phase-randomised inputs with the same power spectrum are cancelled more slowly than the natural EOD-ringing pattern (Fig. 5d). A uniform delay-line basis with similar tuning widths cancels at a comparable rate (Fig. S10).
- **Delayed inputs are essential.** Model granule cells without UBC (late/pause) inputs give "far less effective" cancellation (Fig. 5d, green). Late/pause input probabilities of 3–9% all work; 1% is slower but still far better than none (Fig. S8).
- **A structural prediction confirmed.** Because most granule cells are active early, pairing a dendritic spike at a long delay predicts a *biphasic* change (a dip at the paired time plus a "paradoxical" early depolarisation from non-associative potentiation of the many early inputs). Measured after 3 min (~600 commands) of pairing at 7 delays (n = 3–10 cells per delay), the shapes matched the model after fitting only the two plasticity magnitudes (Fig. 6). A delay-line basis predicts only time-shifted copies of one shape.
- The authors expect the match to survive changes in EOD amplitude over growth and water conductivity because such changes alter "the size rather than the temporal structure" of the self-signal.

## What it means for the design
1. **Self–other separation as a learned subtraction, not a label or a slot.** "Other" is operationally the residual after subtracting what one's own command history predicts. No attribution variable is needed.
2. **The dyadic transposition (mine, derived).** When the partner mirrors the agent, the partner's face is, from the agent's point of view, *reafference*: a delayed, filtered consequence of the agent's own expressions, exactly like the EOD ringing. A negative image built on a temporal basis of the agent's *own* expression commands would cancel the part of the partner's face that is a response to the agent, at whatever delays the basis spans, and leave the partner's own news. At convergence of the LMS form of this rule the residual is uncorrelated with every basis function, so the loop agent → partner-mirrors → agent has zero gain on the span of the basis. This is the structural version of what report 8 asked for (ignoring more than simultaneous movement; delays of minutes).
3. **The paper's numbers say what the basis must contain.** Without delayed elements cancellation fails; the long-delay elements are rare (≈ 5%) but necessary; and the basis can be shaped to the statistics of the self-caused signal. For a dyad, the long-delay end must reach minutes, and should act on slow (low-passed) components, since a sparse set of long pure delays cannot cancel a fast signal copied at a long lag. (Checked in a throwaway script: with a 2-s OU own command, a 0.5-s-lag partner copy is cancelled to the ideal residual, 6.1% vs 5.9% expected; a 40-s-lag copy of the fast signal is left at 79%; a slow 60-s component copied at 40 s or 90 s is left at 46% and 65% after 33 minutes of data.)
4. **The learned weights are themselves a relationship descriptor.** The negative image W is the agent's model of *how this partner responds to it*, per channel and per delay. That is information the project wants (contingency, responsiveness), and it lives in W, not in the drive.
5. **Warning.** Cancelling all partner responses to the agent also removes the signal "you responded to me", which the contingency literature in this project treats as central. The prediction WΦ must therefore be kept as its own signal, not only discarded.

## Verdict
- **adopt (as mechanism for the self/other split; prior art for any reafference-cancellation core).** The mathematics is the adaptive filter (LMS on a fixed basis), so a core built on it is a recombination; the useful structural facts are the random-mixture temporal expansion, the necessity of rare long-delay elements, and the residual's orthogonality to the own-history span.
