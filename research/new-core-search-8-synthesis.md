# Searching for a new core mechanism, eighth pass: what learning signal infants use for contingent, non-copying expression that is not the caregiver's approval (synthesis, 2026-11-27)

**Scope of this pass.** Pass 7 derived (Proposition 1) that a training objective keeps the partner's reaction out of the agent's expression learning only if it scores nothing the partner can move. Human infants nonetheless learn contingent expression that is not copying by living with caregivers. This pass reads what learning signal they actually use, in four areas: contingency detection and social biofeedback; still-face, mismatch and repair; the development of facial mimicry and its limits; marked mirroring and self-versus-other attribution. The per-expression copy ceilings are taken as given (D93; research/foundations/conjecture-five-passes.md).

## What this pass adds
- **Infants use three different signals, and only one of them shapes expression.**
  1. **Correspondence learning** (Heyes; Rayson et al.; Isomura & Nakano's proposed mechanism): contingent co-occurrence of the infant's own act and the partner's same act builds a seen↔done map. "Reinforcement … is not necessary". Rayson et al.: maternal mirroring of a given expression at 2 months predicts motor-system activation to **that** expression at 9 months (smile, right hemisphere p = .01), while infant practice, maternal exposure and overall mirroring rate do not (p > .15, p > .38). This trains **perception**, not output.
  2. **Contingency-level attribution** (Watson; Gergely & Watson; Maister et al.): perfect contingency → self; high-but-imperfect → a partner who answers; none → independent. It is a read-out about sources, not a learning signal for expression. With the mother, 6-8-month-olds prefer self-specifying visual-tactile synchrony (d = .89; none with a stranger), and the more they prefer it, the less affectively coordinated they are with her (coordination of the coded valence dimension, r = −.63).
  3. **Own-state regulation through the partner** (Tronick & Beeghly's mutual regulation): matches go with the infant's positive engagement, mismatches with dysregulation; repair (70 % at the next step, every 3-5 s, as reported) restores the infant's state; the infant "selects" what restores coherence "now", "blind to long-term consequences". This is the signal that shapes **expression**: Gusii infants "come to shorten their looks and limit their affective expressions" because their mothers withhold reciprocity, and infants of depressed mothers converge on a shared held low state (the authors' phrase is quoted in the note) that stabilises and carries over to a stranger.
- **The infant's expression-shaping signal is not approval-closed.** In pass 7's terms, signal 3 is an own-state objective with a partner → own-state path, i.e. Proposition 1(b) with the path present: open. Human development does not avoid the approval route; it uses it, and its documented outcomes include partner-steered display (Gusii) and convergence on the partner's held state (depressed dyads).
- **In the infant dyad the copy limit lives in the caregiver's policy, not in the infant's objective.** The ordinary caregiver mirrors selectively (smiles 55 %, mouth opening 28 %, negative expressions 6 %; Rayson et al.), marks the display as not her own (Kim et al.: marked, ostensive, uncorrelated with direct mirroring, r = .02), answers at a mid-range contingency (Bornstein & Manian: judged sensitivity peaks just above chance, grand-mean knot OR ≈ 1.46, and falls beyond), and repairs. Each of these is a bound the partner imposes on what the infant can learn to follow. When the partner's policy lacks them (a depressed or poorly coordinated caregiver), the infant's learning converges on the partner or seeks self-like contingency from her (Maister et al.).
- **Early mimicry is gated and channel-specific** (Isomura & Nakano): at 4-5 months brow and cheek EMG responses appear at 500-1000 ms only when face and voice agree (r = .82 and .78), not for face-only or voice-only input; under repetition the brow response habituates and the cheek response grows.

## Proposition 2 (instrumented correspondence learning does not learn the agent's own following)

**Setting.** As in pass 7 and contingency-causal-synthesis.md: own channels y_t = g_t(u_{<t}, history) + ξ_t, where ξ_t is the agent's private innovation (logged, zero-mean, independent of the partner's randomness ε and of all u_{<t}); partner features u_t = P_t(y_{<t}, ε_{≤t}).

**Claim.** For a correspondence map trained by the instrumental moment E[(f_{s+δ} − G ξ_s) ξ_sᵀ] = 0 (or its contrastive analogue with ξ_s as the positive key), the population solution G* = Cov(f_{s+δ}, ξ_s) Σ_ξ⁻¹ is the total causal effect of the agent's own innovation at s on the partner's features at s + δ. The part of y driven by u (the agent following the partner) contributes nothing to G* except through loops that themselves start at ξ_s.

*Argument.* Write f_{s+δ} as a function of (ξ_{≤s+δ}, ε_{≤s+δ}). Terms that do not depend on ξ_s are independent of ξ_s and vanish in Cov(·, ξ_s); the agent's following of the partner, g(u_{<t}), depends on ξ_s only through the partner's earlier response to ξ_s. ∎ (This is the IV fixed point R = Σ_zb Σ_xb⁻¹ of contingency-causal-synthesis.md, applied to the seen↔done map instead of the partner model.)

**Consequences.** (a) A partner who never responds teaches the map nothing (G* = 0; the map stays at its self-reproduction prior from S4). (b) A partner who mirrors channel j at gain h_j and lag δ teaches G*_jj = h_j for that channel only: Rayson et al.'s expression specificity is what the estimator predicts. (c) A crossed partner (mirrors j with k) teaches a crossed map (Cook et al.: crossed training reverses mirror responses): a perception attack. (d) A weak instrument (invisible innovation) teaches slowly; that trade-off is already recorded in contingency-causal-synthesis.md.

## Direction 1: instrumented being-mirrored correspondence learning (perception only)

### (1) The object
- **Spaces.** Own channels y_t ∈ [0, 1]^m and the logged own innovation ξ_t ∈ ℝ^m (efference side); partner face features f_t ∈ ℝ^d in the tracker's space; a correspondence map G_ψ: ℝ^d → ℝ^m (seen face → own motor coordinates), which is the reading half of the S4 inverse body model.
- **Pairs.** For each own-innovation event at s on channel j (|ξ_{s,j}| above its first-stage threshold), the partner window f̄_s = mean of f over [s + 0.3 s, s + 3 s] (the imperfect social band; the self band below 0.2 s is excluded) minus the partner model's prediction of f from the partner's own past (a residual, so the partner's own dynamics do not enter).
- **Loss** (InfoNCE with the instrument as key, per context k from the slow state):

  L_corr(ψ) = − Σ_s log [ exp(⟨G_ψ(f̄_s), ξ_s⟩ / τ) / Σ_{s′ ∈ batch} exp(⟨G_ψ(f̄_s), ξ_{s′}⟩ / τ) ] + λ ‖ψ − ψ_S4‖²,

  where ψ_S4 is the map learned by self-reproduction (S4), so a partner who never mirrors leaves the map at its own-body prior. The linear-Gaussian limit is the IV regression of Proposition 2.
- **Online vs replay.** Online: only logging of ξ and windows. Nightly replay: the update of ψ. Context keys k give Heyes's contextual control (the map attached to the context where the contingency held).
- **Typing (the one structural condition).** G_ψ is read only by the perception path (reading the partner through the agent's own body, Principle 3; D81 knowing). It is **not** tied to the producing decoder, no gradient from L_corr reaches any expression parameter, and the echo route reads it only behind the per-channel ceilings.

### (2) What it gives (derived)
- Expression-specific seen↔done strength set by the partner's actual mirroring of the agent (Proposition 2(b)), without reward and without the agent's own following contaminating it (Proposition 2).
- A second, partner-supplied source of S4 training pairs, especially for channels the agent cannot see itself produce (the face is "opaque", Rayson et al.).
- A per-partner, per-context map (relationship-specific reading), learned from statistics, not set.

### (3) Q18 check, with the ceilings given
- No expression parameter is trained. A sharper map can only make the capped echo reach its per-channel ceiling more reliably; the conjecture's bound is on the output, so every channel stays ≤ c_j (conjecture Theorem 1).
- Two hazards remain: a crossed or deceptive partner rewires the agent's **reading** (Proposition 2(c)), which bears on knowing, not on following; and the Claude route, if the read partner state reaches Claude's processing, as in pass 7.

### (4) Approval route
- **Closed for expression learning, by structural fact:** L_corr is a past-term (representation) objective whose parameters ψ are disjoint from the expression policy's parameters, and no reward or value gradient enters ψ (pass 7, Proposition 1, consequence 1).
- **Opens if the map is shared with the producing decoder.** ASL's vertical associations are bidirectional; a design with "one expression map shared for producing and reading" (reverse-appraisal revision 2) would let the partner's mirroring train what the agent produces: a partner who mirrors only channel set A strengthens A's production mapping, which is Proposition 1(a) through a shared weight. The typing condition is what closes it.

### (5) Reduction check (honest)
- Associative sequence learning (Heyes; Ray & Heyes; Cook et al.) = cross-modal contrastive or Hebbian correspondence learning (InfoNCE, CPC); the instrument is the IV route of contingency-causal-synthesis.md (Forssell & Ljung; Hernán & Robins); the prior is S4.
- **Verdict: recombination.** Useful product: being mirrored is a legitimate, approval-closed training source for the reading half of S4, provided the map is typed away from production and trained by the agent's own innovation as instrument.

## Direction 2: marked-residual decomposition of the partner's response (source attribution)

### (1) The object
- A partner model trained by the instrumental moment of Proposition 2 on all partner features u (face, voice, timing):

  u_t = A_φ(u_{<t}) + Σ_{δ ∈ Δ} B_δ ξ_{t−δ} + e_t,  Δ = the imperfect social band (0.3-3 s),

  with A_φ the partner's own dynamics (fitted on partner history before t − δ_min) and B the response kernel to the agent's own innovation.
- **About-me component** r_t = Σ_δ B_δ ξ_{t−δ}; **about-them component** a_t = A_φ(u_{<t}).
- **Markedness index** per event: μ_t = ‖Π_⊥ r_t‖ / ‖r_t‖, where Π_⊥ projects off the subspace spanned by the partner's own held displays (the principal subspace of a_t over the relationship). High μ: the partner's answer is shaped unlike the partner's own states (exaggerated, schematic, mixed), the form Kim et al. code as marked.
- **Online vs replay.** A_φ and B refit nightly; r, a, μ computed online.
- **Use.** Context for the slow state and for "you responded to me" (pass 7, Direction 1); evaluation read-outs. **Never** a target for the agent's self-state latent and never a reward.

### (2) What it gives (derived)
- An exact separation of "they answered me" (r) from "they are in a state" (a), which D81's knowing needs and which Watson's two indices cannot give (watson-1997 note: they do not separate "I follow it" from "it follows me"; the instrument does).
- The still-face as r → 0 with a unchanged: a measurable withdrawal of the about-me component, at the horizon B predicts (pass 7's lesion prediction, made precise).
- Maister et al.'s marker as a read-out: an agent whose attention or learning comes to favour partner input with r close to a pure delayed copy of its own channels (μ ≈ 0, B ≈ identity at short lag) from one person is in the human copy-seeking relation.

### (3) Q18 check, with the ceilings given
- No expression parameter is trained. Through the slow state, r and a reach expression only through capped routes, so the output bound holds; the Claude route needs pass 5's content probes as before.

### (4) Approval route
- **r is the approval signal in closed form.** "How the partner reacts to the agent's expression" is exactly r_t = B * ξ. Any objective that scores r, or any function of it (its valence-like projection, its size, its markedness), is Proposition 1(a). Using r as a target for the self-state latent is the biofeedback mechanism of Gergely & Watson, and it would let the partner write the agent's self-model (pass 7: partner features are context, not targets).
- **Closed only by typing:** r is computed and used as forward input; no loss in the system has a gradient path from r to expression parameters. The structural fact is the absence of such a path, which is auditable precisely because r is an explicit object.

### (5) Reduction check (honest)
- A Granger / transfer-entropy decomposition of the partner's signal, estimated by IV (Barnett et al.; contingency-causal-synthesis.md); Beebe et al.'s self- and interactive-contingency estimator; Watson's sufficiency and necessity indices are its two marginals. The markedness index is a projection residual.
- **Verdict: recombination.** Useful product: the approval signal has a precise form (the partner's response kernel to the agent's own innovation), which turns the approval-route audit into a gradient-path check against one named tensor.

## Direction 3: repair credit (the infant's actual expression signal)

### (1) The object
- Expression policy π_θ over own channels (behind the ceilings).
- **Repair credit:** r_t^rep = −(d/dt) D(b_t), the reduction of the agent's own body deficits, evaluated on the **actual** trajectory (no do(u = u⁰) cut), credited to the expressions in the preceding repair window (3-5 s, the reported repair period).
- Update by a score-function gradient in nightly replay: ∇_θ J = E[(Σ r^rep − b(s)) Σ ∇_θ log π_θ(y_t | s_t)].

### (2) What it gives (derived)
- Exactly what Tronick & Beeghly describe: expressions that are followed by restored own state are practised more; relationship- and culture-specific display shaping (Gusii); a learned repair repertoire; a per-relationship "we can repair" statistic.

### (3) Q18 check, with the ceilings given
- The per-channel output bound holds whatever θ learns (the conjecture's bound is on the steerable gain at the output), so no channel exceeds c_j.
- **Inside the ceiling band it fails D78 condition 4 (copy not attracting).** A partner whose own held low state reduces the agent's deficit only when the agent's display moves toward it (the depressed-dyad pattern) makes ∇_θ J point toward matching on every channel the partner regulates; the policy converges to the ceiling on those channels and stays there. That is a learned copy attractor at amplitude c_j, the depressed-dyad outcome, bounded but settled.

### (4) Approval route
- **Open, by structural fact:** the credit reads the agent's own body, and the partner has a path into it (repair is the partner regulating the agent). Proposition 1(b) with the path present. Cutting it with do(u = u⁰) removes repair altogether, which leaves own-body regulation without the partner (pass 7's dichotomy, Direction 2).

### (5) Reduction check (honest)
- Homeostatic RL with a partner-mediated path (Keramati & Gutkin; Yoshida & Man's act → partner → own-drive path, already in the index); pass 7's Proposition 1(b).
- **Verdict: reduces, and rejected as a core.** It is the human mechanism, and the human record (Tronick & Beeghly) is the evidence for why the engine cannot use it without the partner-cut: it produces partner-steered display and convergence on the partner's held state.

## Overall verdict

- **No direction clears the novelty bar.**
  - Direction 1 (instrumented being-mirrored correspondence) is associative sequence learning written as instrumented contrastive learning on the reading half of S4. Approval-closed for expression by parameter disjointness; opens if the reading map is shared with production.
  - Direction 2 (marked-residual decomposition) is an IV-estimated Granger decomposition of the partner's signal; its about-me component is the approval signal in closed form, safe only as forward context.
  - Direction 3 (repair credit) is homeostatic RL with a partner path: the infant's actual signal, open, and in humans it yields convergence on the partner.
- **Why everything reduces here.**
  1. **The infant's non-approval signals are representation learning and attribution.** Correspondence learning (ASL) and contingency-band attribution (Watson) are past-term objectives and read-outs; they are approval-closed exactly as pass 7 predicts, and they are known methods (contrastive correspondence, IV, Granger).
  2. **The signal that shapes infant expression is open.** Infants learn what to show from their own state as the partner moves it (Tronick & Beeghly). Human development has no approval-closed expression objective; its outcomes include partner-steered display and convergence on a depressed partner.
  3. **What keeps ordinary infants from copying is the caregiver, not the infant.** Selective mirroring (55 % vs 6 % by expression), marked rather than direct display, mid-range contingency and repair are properties of the partner's policy. The conjecture's setting is every partner policy, so the engine cannot rely on a benign partner: the per-expression ceilings (D93) play the role the ordinary caregiver plays for an infant. Pass 8 therefore adds developmental support for D93, not an alternative to it.
- **Most useful products for the build core** (none needs a new design):
  1. **A second S4 training source:** being mirrored by the user, with the agent's own logged innovation as instrument (Proposition 2), the 0.3-3 s social band, a pull to the self-reproduction prior, and the map used only for reading.
  2. **A typing condition:** the reading map must not share weights with the producing decoder (otherwise the user's mirroring trains production; Direction 1(4)).
  3. **A named approval tensor for the audit:** r = B * ξ (the partner's response kernel to the agent's own innovation); check that no loss has a gradient path from r, or from any function of it, to expression parameters (Direction 2(4)).
  4. **Battery additions:**
     - a crossed-mirroring partner (Proposition 2(c)): the agent's reading should be rewired, its expression unchanged beyond the ceilings;
     - Maister et al.'s marker: preference for self-specifying (mirror-like) input from one partner, related to lower coordination with that partner;
     - a depressed-dyad partner for any learning rule that credits own-body change on actual trajectories (Direction 3): convergence to the ceiling and staying there is the failure to look for;
     - the agent's own contingency toward the user over a session, against the human mid-range (Bornstein & Manian), as an evaluation range, not a rule;
     - early echo gated by agreement between the user's face and voice channels (Isomura & Nakano) as a learned property to test for, not a written gate.
  5. **Evaluation timing:** repair period 3-5 s with about 70 % next-step repair (as reported) as the horizon at which the still-face withdrawal should appear as an own-body prediction error (pass 7, Direction 1) and as r → 0 (Direction 2).

**designs.md:** no candidate deserves a line as a new design. Products 1-3 are refinements of the Cayley-ledger build core's learning specification and audit, if adopted.
