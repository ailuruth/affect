# Sato, Fujimura, Kochiyama & Suzuki 2013, "Relationships among Facial Mimicry, Emotional Experience, and Emotion Recognition" (PLoS ONE 8(3):e57889, doi 10.1371/journal.pone.0057889)

Link: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3607589/ (open access; read through the PMC OAI full-text XML because the article page served a bot check).

## What was read
The full text: introduction, methods, results with tables 1–6, discussion. The two original studies whose data are re-analysed (Study 1: Fujimura, Sato & Suzuki 2010, Int J Psychophysiol 76; Study 2: Sato, Fujimura & Suzuki 2008, Int J Psychophysiol 70) were not read.

## What it says
- **Data.** A re-analysis of two EMG studies (N = 38 and 29, Japanese students, mostly women): 32 trials of dynamic (neutral to peak over 0.9–1.5 s) or static faces of positive and negative expressions; corrugator and zygomaticus, mean change over 2.5 s after onset. After the EMG run the stimuli were shown again and rated on the affect grid for the valence and arousal the participant felt (Study 1) and felt and recognised (Study 2).
- **Own face covaries with own felt valence.** For dynamic faces, standardised paths: corrugator → felt valence −0.25 and −0.31; zygomaticus → felt valence +0.27 and +0.30 (Studies 1, 2); felt → recognised valence 0.84. The full path model beat every partial model (AIC 17.1 vs 19.2–36.3). Static faces gave weaker or marginal paths. No paths to arousal.
- **The authors' own caveat.** The analysis cannot establish direction: the face may follow a felt response produced by appraisal rather than cause it. Ratings came from a second viewing, not the EMG trial.

## What matters for this engine
- **Gap 8.** This is the facial-feedback reading of residual same-sign transfer: the more one's own face matched, the more one's own felt valence matched (r about .26–.33). It is correlational, between conditions within people, with ratings taken later; it cannot show that the matching face produced the feeling. It is consistent with, and does not establish, a feedback path.
- **For the build core.** The agent's own rendered face enters only the gates (V_own, with LP and CR masked) and the nightly training of Π̃; it does not change the agent's drives. So the build core has **no facial-feedback path** from its own echo to its own state. If humans' residual transfer were facial feedback, the build core would show same-sign transfer on the face (fast echo) but not in state. A feedback path from the agent's own smile channels to a drive would also make his smiles rewarding through the echo (Principle 6), so it should stay absent; the difference from humans is then a stated, testable departure, not a gap to close.
- **Learning signal:** none studied.

## Limits
Re-analysis; small samples; delayed ratings; dimensional ratings only; only two muscles.
