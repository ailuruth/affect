## Key claims, with numbers
- **Definition.** Empowerment is the channel capacity of the interventional channel from the agent's actuators to its own later sensors: E = C(A_t → S_{t+1}) = max_{p(a)} I(S_{t+1}; A_t) (Eq. 4.7); n-step: action sequences (A_{t+1}…A_{t+n}) to S_{t+n+1}, open-loop. It needs a causal channel p(s | â) (no common cause, no reverse influence). The capacity-achieving p*(a) is **not** the agent's behaviour policy: empowerment is potential, not realised, influence.
- **Deterministic case** (Eq. 4.15): E = log |S_A|, the log of the number of distinguishable sensor states reachable. Maze (Klyubin et al., as reported): 5-step empowerment ranges 3.46–5.52 bits over a 10×10 maze and is negatively correlated with average distance to all other cells; Anthony et al. relate it to graph centrality, with counterexamples when relevant structure lies outside the horizon.
- **Context-dependent empowerment** (Eqs. 4.8–4.12): E(r) = max I(S; A | r); with coarse contexts K, E_free ≤ E(K) ≤ E(R), and a minimal optimal context K_opt = argmin H(K) s.t. E(K) = E(R) is "the context that best characterizes the world in relation to how the agent's actions affect the world" — proposed as a way for an agent to form purely intrinsic contexts from its own sensorimotor capacity.
- **Sensor choice decides the landscape** (Fig. 4.3, from Klyubin et al.): a pushable box the agent cannot sense gives a flat map (E = log₂ 61 ≈ 5.93 bit everywhere); sensing it raises empowerment near the box (5.93–7.79). "Empowerment is about influencing the world one can perceive."
- **"Tragedy of the Greek Gods."** If actions or sensors are much richer than the other (e.g. 100-step horizon, or a sensor that records the agent's own past actions), the landscape goes flat: "for meaningful interaction to emerge … limitations in sensing and acting need to be present."
- **Evolved sensors** concentrate where information is; actuator placement is unspecific; agents' actions are "digested information", denser than the environment, so agents are worth observing (Salge & Polani 2011).
- **Multi-agent** (Capdepuy, as reported): selfish empowerment maximisation gives zero-sum-like, cooperative or Nash-like outcomes depending on the scenario; density-sensing agents form structures (need others near enough to vary input, far enough to keep options).
- **Continuous case.** Noise is required for finite capacity. Quasi-linear Gaussian approximation: s = f(r, a) + N(0, N_r), locally linear T; capacity by SVD and water-filling, C = max Σ_i ½ log(1 + σ_i P_i) s.t. Σ P_i ≤ P (Eq. 4.31), introducing a free power constraint P. Pendulum: greedy empowerment control swings up and balances; with high P the upright state is no longer the maximum ("if in doubt, use a bigger hammer").
- **Admitted limits.** No integration of non-default goals ("fully open"); locality — anything just beyond the horizon is invisible; cost grows exponentially with horizon; continuous approximations add assumptions and parameters. Empowerment is maximised, never satiated; vanishing empowerment is "equivalent to the agent's death".

## The mathematical object
**A state value E(r) = capacity of the agent's own action → own sensor channel in context r; behaviour climbs it (greedily or by planning). Which sensors count is a free design choice that decides the landscape.**

## What it means for the engine
1. **Sensor typing is the lever for the approval route.** If the partner's face is among the sensors, empowerment rewards the agent for being able to change the partner's face in many distinguishable ways: a drive to make the partner maximally responsive to it. That is the partner's reactivity (his attention) as a value, against Principle 6. If the sensors are only the agent's own body variables, the partner enters only as context r of the channel p(b | a, r).
2. **Own-body empowerment is a capacity version of allostatic self-efficacy** (stephan-2016): "how much can my actions still do to my body here" rather than "is my surprise falling". It is not satiable as defined (heterostatic in Oudeyer & Kaplan's sense) and would need a floor to fit Principle 6.
3. **K_opt is a label-free route to contexts.** The minimal context that preserves how one's actions affect one's body would split by person only if a person changes that channel. This is a principled origin for "structure over people" keys, but in a screen dyad the partner rarely changes the agent's own body channel directly.
4. **The Greek-gods result is a warning for rich latents:** an own-body channel measured through a high-capacity learned latent can go flat; empowerment needs coarse sensing.

## Verdict
- **background (formalism, failure modes), reject as core objective.** Read as the reference statement of empowerment. It is a maximised, non-satiable quantity whose social version (partner among the sensors) is an attention drive; the own-body version is used in the pass-7 synthesis (Candidate 2).
