## What it says (abstract)
- Claims the first TTS with explicit token-level control of content duration and pause, through token-level duration conditioning, high-confidence duration supervision and training that is robust to missing controls; evaluated on a timing-control benchmark and on local timing edits.

## What matters for this engine
- Timing only (the RT and PAU channels); pitch and energy would still need WORLD or another path. The spec already sets pauses and rate explicitly (TTS pauses discarded, time-warp to RT).
