## Open gaps every new design must answer

1. **The flat-to-copy dial: the refined rule (operator, Q18; D78).** No single quantity may run the whole face from flat to copy. Each channel's strength must:
   - be set per expression by context and the learned relationship;
   - be able to flip sign;
   - stay under an innate ceiling;
   - never make copying an attracting state.

   Every such quantity must be named and bounded. (In twelve of twelve sketches, one scalar ran the whole face.)
2. **A genuinely new computation,** not a key space, a naming or time scales added to an existing mechanism (Q9 novelty test).
3. **The whole system from the one core:**
   - perception of the other's face and voice through the agent's own body;
   - the latent state where emotion emerges;
   - face and voice output;
   - Claude coupling without text;
   - learning by living;
   - memory and replay.
4. **The brief's expressive-structure items:** content-dependent selection, structure over people, episodes and time, an effective dimensionality, and richness from structure.
5. **The documented failures of existing systems** (known-failures.md).
6. **Human mimicry changes shape, not only size** (research/mimicry-context-synthesis.md): each expression is gated separately, some responses flip sign, fast matching comes before slower context effects, and partner history is learned within minutes. A design must produce this, not just avoid a dial.
7. **Mathematical depth** (operator note Q17, 2026-11-25T10:54:53Z): each design needs
   - a precise object;
   - proved properties (no flat-to-copy setting, or exactly what it has instead; satiable drives; time scales from structure; reading through one's own body as a mathematical statement);
   - a mathematical reason the closest existing mechanisms cannot do it;
   - a derived, buildable network with its budget on this Mac.

   The critique checks the proofs step by step.

8. **The social-appraisal route** (research/social-appraisal-synthesis.md, cycle 2). The partner's expression is treated as evidence about the shared event and the partner's concerns, judged against the agent's own concerns. Per-expression gating and sign flips then follow, and the relationship becomes a set of signed concern weights. Three things a design on this route must still answer:
   - **Where the scalar may hide:** a precision on the face's evidence, one partner-welfare weight, or social referencing when the event is ambiguous. Each needs a guard.
   - **Labels:** the formal models are observer-side and use emotion labels, so it must be grounded without them (Principles 1–2).
   - **Principle 3 is binding:** a fast matching route stays, which fits the fast-match-then-context data, and the leftover same-sign transfer of Parkinson & Simons must be explained.
