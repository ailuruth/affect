# Morise, Yokomori & Ozawa 2016, "WORLD: A vocoder-based high-quality speech synthesis system for real-time applications"

IEICE Transactions on Information and Systems E99-D(7):1877-1884. doi:10.1587/transinf.2015EDP7457.

Source read: the published PDF from J-STAGE (8 pp.), read in full except the reference list; the CheapTrick and PLATINUM equations were partly garbled in text extraction. Also checked: the GitHub repository's license header (mmorise/World) and the Python wrapper (pyworld 0.3.5), which I installed in a throwaway virtual environment and timed on this Mac (below). The LF glottal model (Fant, Liljencrants & Lin 1985, STL-QPSR 4/1985) was NOT read: no copy could be downloaded.

## Claims
- WORLD splits speech into three parameter streams and resynthesizes from them: F0 (DIO: low-pass filter bank, F0 candidates from the agreement of four interval measures (zero crossings, peaks, dips) in each band, most reliable candidate wins); spectral envelope (CheapTrick: pitch-synchronous analysis with a 3·T0 Hanning window, power temporally stabilized, smoothed over 2·ω0/3, then liftering that removes the F0-dependent component and recovers the envelope, q0 = 1.18, q1 = -0.09); and an excitation / aperiodicity stream (PLATINUM in this paper; the later releases use D4C). Synthesis convolves a minimum-phase response from the envelope with the excitation for each glottal pulse, pulse positions set by the F0 contour.
- Compared with Legacy-STRAIGHT and TANDEM-STRAIGHT on four-mora Japanese words with consonants (48 kHz), 10 listeners, MUSHRA: WORLD scored highest (all pairwise p < .0001), with the largest margins on male speech; on female speech it did not differ from Legacy-STRAIGHT.
- Speed: over ten times faster than the other systems in Matlab on a laptop i7; only WORLD had a real-time factor compatible with real-time processing. DIO avoids a per-frame STFT; CheapTrick uses one power spectrum instead of two; synthesis is one convolution per pulse.
- Limits stated by the authors: DIO needs high-SNR speech (fails with additive noise); voiced/unvoiced errors degrade quality; minimum-phase approximation hurts low-pitched voices (phase is audible at low F0); WORLD cannot manipulate the aperiodic parameter as freely as the STRAIGHT systems; pitch-shift quality was not evaluated here.

## Evidence (paper, and measured on this Mac)
- Paper: MUSHRA with 10 listeners; RTF figures only shown graphically.
- Measured here (Apple M1 Pro, pyworld 0.3.5 under Python 3.15, 5 ms frames, one 4.57 s sentence rendered by macOS `say` at 22.05 kHz): F0 (DIO + StoneMask) 23 ms, CheapTrick 30 ms, D4C 110 ms, synthesis 34 ms. Analysis plus synthesis RTF 0.043; synthesis alone RTF 0.007. Synthesizing 100 ms chunks of frames independently took 0.74 ms each (chunk-boundary artefacts not checked).
- A manipulation pass (pitch +3 semitones, F0 excursions around the log-mean widened by 1.5x, spectral envelope tilted upward with frequency, aperiodicity raised) changed median F0 from 166.7 to 201.1 Hz and F0 SD from 24.2 to 42.5 Hz, and resynthesized in 38 ms. Perceptual quality of the manipulated speech was not judged.
- pyworld 0.3.5 imports `pkg_resources`, which needs setuptools < 81 on current Python; a packaging wart, not a blocker.

## What it implies for an engine whose voice is driven from an internal state through an innate vocal body
- WORLD is a usable innate vocal body for S2 and beyond: a source (F0 contour, pulse timing, aperiodicity = breathiness or noise) and a filter (spectral envelope = tract resonances and spectral tilt), all continuous, all settable per 5 ms frame, and fast enough that the fast loop can re-render prosody faster than real time. That matches the acoustic dimensions that carry affect (banse-scherer-1996-acoustic-profiles.md, scherer-2003-vocal-communication-review.md): F0 level and range, loudness, tempo (frame timing), spectral balance, breathiness and roughness.
- A concrete path that keeps words with Claude and voice with the engine: render the words with the macOS voice (macos-speech-synthesis.md), analyse once with WORLD, and let the engine's state drive the resynthesis (F0 contour shape and range, frame-rate warping for tempo and lengthening, envelope tilt for tense vs lax, aperiodicity for breathy vs pressed, envelope frequency warping for tract length and lip spreading). Analysis takes about 0.16 s for a 4.6 s sentence, so this adds little latency once the TTS audio exists. The same body can make non-speech sounds (hums, back-channel "mm", sighs) by synthesizing directly from engine-generated F0, envelope and aperiodicity without TTS.
- What WORLD is not: a physiological voice source. Glottal settings (open quotient, return phase, pressed vs breathy adduction) are folded into the envelope tilt and the aperiodicity, not exposed as source parameters; Scherer's "tense", "lax", "thin", "full" voices must be approximated by envelope tilt plus aperiodicity plus F0. A parametric glottal model (the LF model, unread here) would give those settings directly but needs its own filter and is lower in naturalness; this is the trade-off to decide when the voice body is built.
- Voice identity stays fixed by the source TTS voice and the engine's envelope settings (Principle 8: the operator chooses the voice). WORLD also makes voice cloning trivial; using the operator's own envelope would violate the spirit of Principles 3 and 7 and should be excluded by design.
- Perception side: DIO's noise sensitivity means WORLD is not the right pitch tracker for the operator's live microphone; keep pitchy (pitchy.md) for perception and use WORLD only on the engine's own clean audio.
