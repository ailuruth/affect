# Task-dependent and distinct roles of the temporoparietal junction and inferior frontal cortex in the control of imitation (Hogeveen et al.)

Jeremy Hogeveen, Sukhvinder S. Obhi, Michael J. Banissy, Idalmis Santiesteban, Clare Press, Caroline Catmur & Geoffrey Bird, *Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience* 10(7):1003–1009, 2015, (PMC4483570; DOI not verified in this session).

## The computation, precisely
- **Design (abstract):** anodal tDCS over right inferior frontal cortex (IFC), over right TPJ, or sham. Three measures: (a) an imitation-inhibition task; (b) imitation during a social interaction, where copying is appropriate; (c) a non-imitative control task.
- **Result (abstract):** IFC stimulation "improved the context-dependent control of imitation". Imitation went **up** in social interaction and **down** in the inhibition task. TPJ stimulation reduced imitation **only** in the inhibition task, "without affecting imitation during social interaction". Neither site affected the non-imitative control task. Later papers read this as TPJ "indirectly" and IFC "directly" involved.

## What it means for the designs
- **Two separable operators, causally dissociated:**
  1. **Self–other partition (TPJ-like).** Enhancing it lowers copying when copying is wrong, and leaves appropriate social imitation alone. It separates; it does not set a level.
  2. **Context-dependent routing (IFC-like).** Enhancing it makes imitation *more context-dependent in both directions*. It is a signed gain whose sign is set by context. This is the STORM policy (wang-hamilton-2012-storm-mimicry.md), localised.
- **Mathematically:** a stronger partition shrinks only the off-diagonal leak from the other-slot to the self-slot. A stronger router increases $|W(\text{context})|$ on both sides of zero. Neither is a flat-to-copy dial, provided the router's range is bounded and its sign is context-set. Together they show that "more control" in the brain is not "less mimicry". It is mimicry that is *more conditional*. That is the shape-not-size result in mimicry-context-synthesis.md, at the level of mechanism.
- **Time scale:** within trial; tDCS effects last tens of minutes.

## Verdict
**adopt (abstract-level; re-read).** It is the best causal evidence that partition and context-routing are different operators, and that strengthening control makes imitation more conditional, not smaller.
