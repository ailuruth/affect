# Unraveling dyadic psychophysiology of social presence between strangers during an audio drama (Kauttonen, Paekivi, Kauramäki & Tikka)

Citation: J. Kauttonen, S. Paekivi, J. Kauramäki, P. Tikka. "Unraveling dyadic psychophysiology of social presence between strangers during an audio drama – a signal-analysis approach." Frontiers in Psychology 14:1153968 (2023). doi:10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1153968.

Source read: publisher PDF, text-extracted. Read in part: abstract, introduction and hypotheses, the ISC/RSA/RQA methods, the classification results and discussion. Not read in full: the detailed RSA scans and appendix figures.

## What it does
- 36 women listened to a 15-min audio drama alone (18) or facing a stranger (18, 9 pairs), filmed by Kinect; facial signals: gaze, eyebrow, smile.
- Inter-subject correlation (ISC) was *lower* in paired listeners than in single listeners, whether computed on true pairs or any two paired individuals: a co-present stranger makes responses more individual, not more synchronous.
- Recurrence quantification of each person's own signals (recurrence rate fixed at 3 %), with gradient boosting, classified paired vs single listeners at 67 % (subject-wise split) and 75 % (random split) against 54 % chance; entropy-type recurrence features ranked highest.

## What it means for the design
1. **Social presence shows up as changed recurrence structure of one's own face, not as more synchrony.** That is a within-person, own-dynamics effect of the other's presence, which the commutator route (driven only by same-channel lead–lag) does not carry; in the design it would have to come through the 6-d body route.
2. Recurrence analysis here is per person, not cross-recurrence; it is a weak precedent for the commutator array and is noted only for the presence effect.

## Verdict
- [Dyadic facial recurrence under social presence (Kauttonen et al.)](kauttonen-2023-dyadic-facial-rqa.md) — 2023 — watch — a stranger's presence lowered inter-subject correlation of gaze, brow and smile and changed each listener's own recurrence structure (paired vs alone classified at 67–75 % vs 54 % chance); presence alters one's own dynamics rather than raising synchrony.
