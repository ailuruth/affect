## Findings / the computation, precisely

**The claim: pleasure is the common currency.** "because behavior is a final common path, various motivations competing for access to it must be able to 'talk to one another' ... there must exist in the brain a common currency". From earlier work (stated, not re-tested here): with conflicting motives "from physiological (hunger, fatigue, thermal discomfort, pain) to psychological (eagerness to play a video-game, to earn, or to save money)", subjects resolved the conflict by "maximizing the bi-dimensional sum of pleasures"; "the final decision was reached according to the algebraic sum of the common currency, pleasure plus displeasure". And: "The optimization of behavior (from a physiological point of view) was demonstrated previously to be based on the maximization of sensory pleasure."

**Experiments (n = 12 each).**
1. Pleasure ratings of 23 classic dilemma items (trolley, vaccination, compensation) matched other studies' decision rates: Spearman 0.85, Kendall 0.75 (p < 0.001); neurosurgery-goodness ratings Spearman 0.82.
2. 50 daily-life situations × 4 actions: the chosen action coincided with the highest-rated pleasure well above the 25 % chance level (t = 16.56); worst-rated actions were chosen 0.96 ± 0.32 times in 50 (< 2 %). Of 3.91/20 unethical choices, 64 % were the subject's top-pleasure option.
3. 50 arithmetic problems × 4 solutions: choices matched top pleasure above chance (e.g. 0.74 vs 0.26 for subject 1; Wilcoxon p = 0.004), including *wrong* answers (p = 0.006), and more so in better mathematicians (r = 0.61).

**State-dependence of the currency.** Ratings drifted down with fatigue over the 200-item session (items 11–20: +1.7 ± 0.98; items 181–190: −2.15 ± 1.13; p = 0.031), and yet "this influence did not suppress the gradients of hedonicity" across the options. That is the alliesthesia structure on a small scale: the internal state shifts the level of the currency while preserving within-item ranking.

**Limits stated by the authors.** Correlation, not causation: "the present results do not prove the point under test"; they satisfy "a necessary condition". Ratings may reflect expected rather than experienced pleasure. Small samples; questionnaire paradigms; no physiology.

## What it means for the designs
- **One signed scalar readout, summed across drives, is defensible.** The agent does not need named emotion variables to trade off motives: a single algebraic sum of the per-drive rate terms (relief positive, rising load negative) is Cabanac's common currency. In the Cayley ledger this is the valence v = −Σ_i w_i dD_i/dt (see joffily-coricelli-2013-valence-free-energy.md), shown on the face by Π₀ and given to Claude as a number, not a label.
- **Pleasure is need-dependent; this is what keeps positive affect satiable.** Cabanac's alliesthesia (cited here via Fredrickson 2001 and berridge-2004-motivation-concepts.md, not read in the 1971 original) says the same stimulus is pleasant when it corrects a deficit and unpleasant beyond it. For the agent: the pleasure of a reunion, of good news or of play scales with the agent's own current deficit (K, C, P) and is zero or negative once sated. A partner who keeps sending warmth after the agent is sated produces no further gain — Principle 6 in hedonic form.
- **Use it in draft selection.** Claude's drafts are already selected by J with self-simulated relief (§3.13). Cabanac's result supports scoring drafts by the agent's own predicted pleasure sum (own drive relief), not by predicted partner reaction.
- **Limits.** Weak evidence by modern standards; it supports the common-currency architecture, not any specific equation; the core alliesthesia experiments (1971) remain unread here.
