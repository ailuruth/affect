## The computations, precisely

The review's frame: a neuron maps ~$10^3$ input spike trains to one output, so "an essential feature of this transformation is the amount of input information that must be discarded"; the question is whether "decisions [are] taken only at the final step of transforming the somatic voltage into an action potential, or are there decision points on the way". The toolkit:

1. **Division by shunting inhibition, with a locality condition.** "Shunting inhibition usually describes inhibition that changes the total conductance of the membrane but does not cause any voltage change when activated on its own ... it is more convenient to think of the inhibition as reducing the input resistance of the cell, effectively reducing the voltage response to excitatory current. This property of inhibition can be mathematically formulated as having a divisive effect on excitatory signals (Fatt & Katz 1953)". The crucial structural part: "When excitatory and inhibitory inputs are widely separated from each other on different dendritic branches, then the inputs will tend to sum linearly at the soma. In contrast, when the excitatory and inhibitory inputs are located adjacent to each other, then the inhibition can produce a highly nonlinear 'shunting' of the excitatory input (Rall 1964)". So the *same* inhibitory synapse is either additive or divisive **depending on where it lands**, and it is effective "when it is located on the path between the excitatory input and the soma". Division is also a *vetoing of a subtree*, not a global scale.
   Also: passive sublinear summation "provides a mechanism for saturation of inputs, thus preventing overexcitation of the neuron by a group of synapses".

2. **A two-layer network inside one cell (Mel/Poirazi subunits).** "Mel and colleagues ... have developed a framework that breaks the dendrites into many tiny computational units. The basic nonlinearity in individual branchlets is based on the NMDA spike ... and is modeled as a sigmoidal function. Each subunit thus integrates its inputs and passes them through a sigmodial nonlinearity function ... The picture that emerges from this analysis is of a two-layered neuronal network that resides within a single neuron." Local spikes are generated by input "sufficiently clustered in space and time"; the spike's "spatial extent is limited to a short region of the dendrite by active and passive mechanisms". Verified by model ("the predictive power of the two-layer neural network description is very good", Poirazi et al. 2003b) and by experiment in L5 pyramidal cells (Polsky et al. 2004).

3. **Coincidence detection across compartments, with a ~10 ms window.** Pairing back-propagating APs with distal input triggers "highly nonlinear amplification" (Stuart & Häusser 2001); "This form of coincidence detection exhibits a narrow time window (∼10 ms)". And in L5 cells, distal tuft input coinciding with a back-propagating AP "results in a large dendritic Ca+ spike, which in turn propagates toward the soma and drives the axon to fire a burst of action potentials ... enables the detection of coincident activation of synaptic inputs to the two major compartments of the dendritic tree ... reporting simultaneous activity across different cortical layers. This coincidence detection mechanism is potentially tunable, either by changing dendritic geometry or by modulating channel densities."

4. **Back-propagation makes the single unit a closed loop.** "Computationally this 'backpropagation' has major consequences because it implies that the neuron is no longer an open-loop system but has an internal mechanism of feedback. It is thus no longer the case that feedback is a property only of the network, but rather it is a salient property of each element of the network." The soma–dendrite interaction "can be captured by a reduced, two-compartment model ... and critically depends on the coupling coefficient between the two compartments", which is set by morphology and channel distribution: "The firing patterns of neurons are thus potentially tunable simply by changing dendritic properties."

5. **A spatial integrator: the position of a bistable front holds the integral.** This is the most unusual computation in the review. "Recently, Loewenstein & Sompolinsky (2003) presented a model in which a specifically dendritic bistability enables the dendrite to become a time integrator. In this model, the dendritic concentration of Ca2+ is bistable. If at one end of the dendrite it is forced to be in the up state, and at the other edge to be in the down state, then a standing wave of Ca2+ concentration is created. **The location of the wavefront represents the result of the integral such that incoming transient synaptic input repositions the location of the front.** As long as no input arrives, the front will keep its position and the dendrite will preserve the 'working memory.'" The question it answers is stated exactly: "How does a system integrate transients and maintain the computed integral for a long period, far longer than its intrinsic time constant?"

6. **Blurring by dendro-dendritic coupling as an image operation.** Fly HS/vCH cells are electrically coupled via dendritic gap junctions so that a retinotopic motion map "is blurred in the dendrites of the CH cell via dendro-dendritic connections", and the blurred copy is subtracted ("conveying inhibitory dendro-dendritic input to FD cells, being subtracted from the retinotopic input, an enhancement of the motion edges is achieved"). "the biophysical properties of dendritic trees can implement non-trivial image processing operations in a simple and elegant manner."

7. **Multiplication implemented by segregated inputs.** Locust LGMD: its peak firing time "comes with a fixed delay after the time at which a looming object reaches a fixed-threshold angular size ... independent of the object's approach speed or size", explained as "a multiplication of two parameters ... its angular size and speed of approach", with "The synaptic input ... segregated" onto distinct subtrees.

## Evidence with numbers
Sparse: a ~10 ms coincidence window; a ~50 ms frame as the computational cycle; "hundreds to thousands" of inputs; the HCN1-knockout behavioural result (Nolan et al. 2004) where "distal perforant path synaptic inputs are selectively enhanced ... and that LTP of these inputs is also enhanced, whereas LTP at the more proximal Schaffer collateral input is unchanged". Most quantities are in the cited primary papers, not here. This is a review; it is not a source of measured failure curves.

## Is this a new computation relative to the excluded frameworks?

Parts: (i) cable equation = a linear operator with spatial structure (**excluded**); (ii) sigmoidal subunits summed at a soma = a two-layer MLP (**excluded**, and the authors say so: "two-layer neural networks are general-purpose computation machines"); (iii) divisive shunting = normalisation, i.e. a gain (**excluded**); (iv) coincidence detection = multiplication/AND (**excluded**); (v) local feedback = recurrence (**excluded**); (vi) bistable front = a bistable PDE whose marginal mode is a position — this is a **continuous attractor in space**, close to but not identical with the excluded list (attractors as such are not on the operator's exclusion list; the line-attractor notes in this cycle cover the same object).

Two things survive the test as *not* reducible to a gain, even though their mathematics is classical:

- **(iii) with locality:** the effect of an inhibitory input is not a number, it is a *number plus an address*. The same synapse divides or adds depending on whether it is on the path between a given excitation and the soma. "Strength" and "which inputs it acts on" are separate degrees of freedom, and the second is discrete.
- **(vi):** the stored variable is a **location**, not an amplitude, and its persistence does not come from a time constant: "far longer than its intrinsic time constant". A perturbation moves the front and the front stays. This is the one computation in the review whose state variable is not a magnitude.

## What it suggests for an affective core where the partner is structured, not a gain

1. **The partner as an address, not an amplitude (the shunting result, made exact).** Give the partner channel no access to magnitudes and only the power to place a divisive term *at a location* in the agent's own expressive tree. Then partner influence is characterised by the pair (where, with the agent's own fixed strength), and the reachable set of effects is a set of subtree vetoes — finite, enumerable, and provably without a flat-to-copy path, because no placement can synthesise "become the partner's value": shunting can only remove the agent's own contributions. A mathematically checkable statement: a divisive term on the path from subtree $S$ to the output can only shrink $S$'s contribution toward zero, so its image is contained in the convex hull of the agent's own outputs with that subtree silenced. **Copying is outside the image.**
2. **The front position as the affective state (the strongest idea here).** Let the persistent affective variable be the *position* of a bistable front in a one-dimensional field, with the agent's own events moving it. Persistence is then structural (bistability) rather than a tuned leak, which answers the brief's "time scales from structure" requirement and gives the same object the hypothalamic line-attractor notes in this cycle arrive at from a different direction (`nair-2023-vmhvl-line-attractor.md`, `vinograd-2024-causal-line-attractor.md`). The partner can be allowed to *displace* the front only through the agent's own transients; the expression mapping is read off the front's position.
3. **Two integration zones with a coincidence requirement** means the partner's evidence (one compartment) cannot produce output without the agent's own ongoing drive (the other compartment) — an AND, not a sum. That is a structural way to state "reading the other through one's own body" and it has a measurable signature: with the agent's own drive at zero, no partner input of any strength produces expression.
4. **Blur-and-subtract** is a candidate for "what the agent expects the partner to do" without any model of the partner: a spatially smoothed copy of the agent's own perceptual map, subtracted, leaves only the discrepancy. No partner parameters are learned.
5. **A caution.** The two-layer-subunit framing is the part of this literature most likely to collapse into "an MLP with extra words"; a design must not use it as its novelty claim.

## Verdict
**adopt (two mechanisms only: place-not-strength shunting, and the bistable-front positional integrator); reject as a novelty source otherwise.** Most of the dendritic toolkit is a linear operator plus sigmoids plus multiplication, all on the excluded list, and the review's own framing ("two-layered neuronal network ... within a single neuron") concedes it.
